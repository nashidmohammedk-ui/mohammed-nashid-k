# Mohammed Nashid K — Creative Portfolio

> **AI Content Creator | Visual Storyteller**

A modern, minimalist portfolio website crafted for **Mohammed Nashid K**, designed with a clean **white and light blue** color palette, refined typography, and generous whitespace.

---

## 🎨 Minimalist White & Light Blue Theme

- **Color Palette**:
  - Pure Canvas White: `#FFFFFF`
  - Subtle Cool Surface: `#F8FAFC`
  - Ice Blue Tint & Glow: `#F0F7FF`, `#E0F2FE`, `#BAE6FD`
  - Sky Blue Accent: `#0EA5E9` & `#38BDF8`
  - Deep Slate Charcoal: `#0F172A` & `#475569`
- **Key Design Signatures**:
  - **Minimal Ambient Waves**: Light-blue frosted aura shapes with delicate sky-blue contour strokes.
  - **Landmark Title**: `P[=====]rtfolio` with an elongated capsule/pill outline `o` and `2026` with a sky-blue brush underline.
  - **Crown & Doodles**: Sky-blue crown doodle with *"Ideas / Design / Impact"* and directional arrow.
  - **Portrait Stage**: Cutout portrait of Mohammed Nashid K in his black hoodie with soft blue aura and interactive 3D mouse parallax.
  - **Tool Badges**: Crisp minimal rounded square badges: `[Ps]`, `[Ai]`, `[Ae]`, `[Pr]`, `[Canva]`.
  - **Minimalist Hairlines**: Clean border definitions and sky-blue sparkles `✦`.
